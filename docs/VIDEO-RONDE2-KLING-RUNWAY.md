# Oefenvideo's: copy-paste prompts

Per oefening één prompt. Kopieer, plak in Kling of Runway (tekst naar video, 10 seconden, 16:9), klaar.
Stuur de video die goed is naar Claude met de naam van de oefening.

## 1. Gestrekt been heffen

```
Physiotherapy exercise demonstration video, 10 seconds, 16:9. Static camera on a tripod at mat height, exact side view, the whole body always fully in frame. The same athletic woman in plain dark olive sportswear lies on her back on a beige exercise mat in a calm warm sand-coloured physiotherapy studio, soft daylight. Her far knee is bent with that foot flat on the mat; the leg closest to the camera lies fully straight on the mat. The foot of the straight leg is flexed, toes pulled up towards the shin, the whole time. She holds still for 2 seconds, then tightens the thigh of the straight leg and slowly lifts the straight leg only about 45 degrees, exactly to the height of the bent knee and never higher, holds there for 2 seconds and lowers it slowly back to the mat. The lifted knee stays completely straight, the toes stay pulled up, the bent leg and the pelvis stay still. Exactly 2 slow, controlled repetitions, then she rests. Realistic anatomy and joint angles. No camera movement, no zoom, no cuts, no other people, no text, no logo, no music, no extra limbs, no morphing.
```
